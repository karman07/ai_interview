# How explicitly explain Big O Cognitive Maps during Live Walkthroughs

## Introduction
Explaining Big O cognitive maps during live walkthroughs is a crucial aspect of technical interviews, particularly for software engineering and data science positions. Big O notation is a measure of an algorithm's complexity, and being able to articulate your thought process and problem-solving approach can make or break your chances of acing the interview. In this article, we will delve into the world of Big O cognitive maps and provide you with practical tips on how to explicitly explain them during live walkthroughs.

## Core Points
To effectively explain Big O cognitive maps, keep the following key points in mind:
* **Start with the basics**: Begin by defining what Big O notation is and its importance in measuring algorithmic complexity.
* **Use simple examples**: Illustrate the concept of Big O using simple, relatable examples, such as searching for an element in an array or sorting a list of numbers.
* **Visualize the problem**: Use diagrams or whiteboarding to visualize the problem and demonstrate how the algorithm works.
* **Break down the solution**: Break down the solution into smaller, manageable parts, and explain each step in detail.
* **Discuss trade-offs**: Discuss the trade-offs between different algorithms and data structures, highlighting the pros and cons of each approach.
* **Practice, practice, practice**: Practice explaining Big O cognitive maps with sample problems and mock interviews to build your confidence and fluency.

## Golden Rules / Pro Tips
To take your explanation to the next level, follow these golden rules:
🔍 **Be clear and concise**: Avoid using jargon or overly complex language, and focus on conveying your message in a simple, straightforward way.
💡 **Use analogies and metaphors**: Use analogies and metaphors to help illustrate complex concepts and make them more relatable.
📝 **Write clean code**: Write clean, readable code that is easy to understand, and use comments to explain your thought process.
🤔 **Think aloud**: Think aloud and explain your thought process as you work through the problem, highlighting any assumptions or uncertainties.
📊 **Use metrics and benchmarks**: Use metrics and benchmarks to demonstrate the performance and efficiency of your solution.
