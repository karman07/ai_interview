# How explicitly explain Big O Cognitive Maps during Live Walkthroughs

## Introduction
Explaining complex technical concepts, such as Big O notation and cognitive maps, in a clear and concise manner is a crucial skill for any aspiring software developer or technical professional. During live walkthroughs, it's essential to be able to break down these concepts into easy-to-understand pieces, making it simpler for the interviewer to follow your thought process. In this article, we'll delve into the world of Big O cognitive maps and explore how to explain them effectively during live walkthroughs.

## Core Points
To explicitly explain Big O cognitive maps during live walkthroughs, keep the following key points in mind:
* **Define Big O notation**: Start by explaining what Big O notation is and its importance in measuring the complexity of algorithms.
* **Visualize the cognitive map**: Describe how a cognitive map can be used to visualize the relationships between different components of an algorithm, making it easier to understand and analyze.
* **Explain time and space complexity**: Break down the concepts of time and space complexity, and how they relate to Big O notation.
* **Provide examples**: Use simple examples to illustrate how Big O cognitive maps can be applied to real-world problems, making it easier for the interviewer to understand the concept.
* **Use analogies and metaphors**: Utilize analogies and metaphors to explain complex technical concepts in a more relatable and engaging way.

## Golden Rules / Pro Tips
To take your explanation to the next level, follow these golden rules and pro tips:
🔍 **Be concise**: Avoid using overly complex language or jargon that might confuse the interviewer.
📝 **Use simple diagrams**: Create simple diagrams or illustrations to help visualize the cognitive map and make the explanation more engaging.
👥 **Encourage interaction**: Encourage the interviewer to ask questions and engage in a discussion, making the explanation more interactive and dynamic.
📊 **Provide examples from experience**: Use real-world examples from your own experience to demonstrate how Big O cognitive maps can be applied in practice.
💡 **Show enthusiasm and passion**: Display your passion and enthusiasm for the subject matter, making the explanation more engaging and memorable.

## Conclusion
Mastering the art of explaining Big O cognitive maps during live walkthroughs takes practice, patience, and dedication. By following the core points, golden rules, and pro tips outlined in this article, you'll be well on your way to becoming a proficient explainer of complex technical concepts. Remember to stay concise, use simple diagrams, and encourage interaction, and you'll be sure to make a lasting impression on your interviewer. With persistence and practice, you'll become a skilled communicator of technical ideas, setting yourself up for success in your future career.
