# Building your first CI/CD boundary test pipeline from scratch bundles

## Introduction
Creating a Continuous Integration/Continuous Deployment (CI/CD) pipeline is a crucial skill for any developer or DevOps engineer. It helps to automate the testing, building, and deployment of software applications, making the development process more efficient and reliable. In this article, we will guide you through the process of building your first CI/CD boundary test pipeline from scratch bundles, which can be a valuable addition to your resume and skill set.

## Core Points
To build a CI/CD boundary test pipeline, you'll need to consider the following key points:
* **Choose a CI/CD tool**: Select a suitable CI/CD tool such as Jenkins, GitLab CI/CD, or CircleCI that fits your needs and integrates well with your development environment.
* **Set up a version control system**: Use a version control system like Git to manage your codebase and track changes.
* **Write automated tests**: Create automated tests for your application to ensure it works as expected and catch any bugs or errors.
* **Configure the pipeline**: Set up the pipeline to automate the build, test, and deployment process, including any necessary dependencies or environments.
* **Monitor and optimize**: Continuously monitor the pipeline's performance and optimize it as needed to improve efficiency and reliability.

## Golden Rules / Pro Tips
Here are some golden rules and pro tips to keep in mind when building your CI/CD boundary test pipeline:
🔍 **Keep it simple**: Start with a simple pipeline and gradually add complexity as needed.
📊 **Use metrics and logging**: Use metrics and logging to monitor the pipeline's performance and identify areas for improvement.
🚀 **Automate everything**: Automate as much of the pipeline as possible to minimize manual intervention and reduce errors.
👥 **Collaborate with the team**: Collaborate with your development team to ensure the pipeline meets their needs and integrates well with their workflows.
📚 **Document everything**: Document the pipeline's configuration, tests, and deployment process to make it easier to maintain and troubleshoot.

## Conclusion
Building a CI/CD boundary test pipeline from scratch bundles can seem daunting, but by following these core points and golden rules, you can create a robust and efficient pipeline that streamlines your development process and boosts your resume. Remember to keep it simple, automate everything, and collaborate with your team to ensure the pipeline meets their needs. With practice and experience, you'll become proficient in creating CI/CD pipelines and be well on your way to advancing your career in the tech industry.
