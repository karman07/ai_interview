# Overcoming Interview Anxiety: Mental Prep & Practice

Hyperventilating before a design round or system walkthrough is incredibly common. Left unchecked, anxiety interferes with working memory retrieval, making it hard to vocalize solutions you otherwise know by heart. 

Here are three validated frameworks to center your cognitive balance.

## 🧘‍♂️ 1. Box Breathing (The 4x4 Rule)
Reset your parasympathetic nervous system 10 minutes before the call:
1. Inhale through nose (4 seconds)
2. Hold breath (4 seconds)
3. Exhale through mouth (4 seconds)
4. Hold empty (4 seconds)
*Repeat 4 times to drop heart rate and cortisol streams instantly.*

## 🗣️ 2. Convert Anxiety into Excitement
Physiologically, anxiety and excitement triggers the exact same adrenaline spikes. The key difference is your mental framing.
- **Instead of thinking**: "I am terrified of failing."
- **Tell yourself**: "I am energized to show my skills and solve problems."

## 🔁 3. Practice Without Judgment (The AI Advantage)
Interviews are performance arts. The best way to build confidence is by eliminating elements of the unknown.
Running mock sessions on **AI for Job** grants a consequence-free buffer environment where you can fail and adapt until responding feels like muscle memory.

Stay focused on the present question rather than downstream outcomes. You've got this!
