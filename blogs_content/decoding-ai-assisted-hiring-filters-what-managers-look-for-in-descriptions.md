# Decoding AI-assisted hiring filters: What managers look for in descriptions

## Introduction
The hiring landscape has undergone a significant transformation with the advent of AI-assisted hiring filters. These filters use algorithms to sift through resumes and descriptions, identifying the most suitable candidates for a particular role. As a job seeker, understanding what managers look for in descriptions can significantly improve your chances of landing an interview. In this article, we will delve into the world of AI-assisted hiring filters and explore the key elements that managers look for in descriptions.

## Core Points
To increase your visibility in AI-assisted hiring filters, consider the following key points:
* **Keyword optimization**: Incorporate relevant keywords from the job description into your resume and description to improve your match score.
* **Quantifiable achievements**: Use specific numbers and metrics to demonstrate your accomplishments and impact in previous roles.
* **Relevant skills**: Ensure that your description highlights the skills and qualifications that align with the job requirements.
* **Clear and concise language**: Use simple and straightforward language to convey your message, avoiding jargon and technical terms that may confuse the AI algorithm.
* **Consistency**: Maintain a consistent tone and format throughout your description to make it easier for the AI filter to parse and understand.

## Golden Rules / Pro Tips
To further enhance your description and increase your chances of passing through AI-assisted hiring filters, follow these golden rules:
📝 **Tailor your description to the job**: Customize your description for each job application, highlighting the skills and experiences that align with the job requirements.
📊 **Use data and statistics**: Incorporate data and statistics to demonstrate your achievements and impact, making it easier for the AI algorithm to quantify your value.
📈 **Showcase your achievements**: Instead of just listing your job responsibilities, focus on the specific achievements and results you've achieved in your previous roles.
📊 **Keep it up-to-date**: Regularly update your description to reflect your latest skills, experiences, and achievements.

## Conclusion
AI-assisted hiring filters are here to stay, and understanding what managers look for in descriptions can give you a significant edge in the job market. By incorporating keyword optimization, quantifiable achievements, relevant skills, clear and concise language, and consistency into your description, you can increase your visibility and improve your chances of landing an interview. Remember to tailor your description to the job, use data and statistics, showcase your achievements, and keep it up-to-date to stay ahead of the competition. With these tips and strategies, you'll be well on your way to decoding AI-assisted hiring filters and landing your dream job.
