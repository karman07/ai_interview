# How to Ace Your AI-Powered Mock Interview

AI-driven mock interviews are revolutionized the way candidates prepare for job placements. Whether you're practicing for a behavioral screening or deep technical DSA rounds, interacting with an AI interface requires a distinct blend of natural dialogue and deterministic structuring.

## 🎙️ 1. Vocal Clarity and Pace
Unlike human recruiters, AI relies heavily on Speech-to-Text (STT) parsing. Mumbling or repeating fragments can introduce transcription static.
* **Practice Pacing**: Maintain a steady pace (~130 words per minute).
* **Pronunciation**: Enunciate technical keywords (e.g., *Microservices*, *Binary Tree*) clearly.

## 🧩 2. Structuring Responses with STAR
The Situational-Task-Action-Result method is your best friend. AI engines look for dimensional benchmarks:
1. **Background Context**: Keep it to 15% of your talk track.
2. **Action Vectors**: Highlight **what you did** rather than what the team did.
3. **Metric Outcomes**: Quantifiable results (e.g., "boosted latency speeds by 40%").

## 💻 3. Live Coding and Commentary
During coding walkthroughs, ensure you comment on your cognitive map out loud. AI metrics evaluate critical-thinking workflows based strictly on the comments you explain while navigating algorithms.
